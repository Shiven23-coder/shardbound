## 0.4.0 (2026-11-22)


### ⚠ BREAKING CHANGES

* Mode COMBAT is now BATTLE, /api/combat/action is replaced by /api/battle/action, HudView has fp/maxFp/shards instead of energy and xp, and mutating endpoints answer 400 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REQUIRED without the header.
* GameView is now SessionView, /api/game/choose moved to /api/dialogue/choose, and saves are format 2.
* saves from the old normalised schema are not migrated (none exist outside development); error bodies are now ProblemDetail.
