# Credits

Every third-party asset used by Shardbound is listed here, in the **same commit** that adds it.
CI fails if a file under `content/art/`, `content/audio/`, `frontend/src/assets/` or `frontend/public/` has no row below (from V3).

## Rules (V2_SPEC §11)
1. **One art pack family and one tile size (16×16 at an integer scale, P-29):** Ninja Adventure by pixel-boy. Never mixed with another family.
2. **No AI-generated art or audio.**
3. Art: **CC0** only. Audio: **CC0 or CC-BY**. Fonts: **SIL OFL 1.1**.
4. CC-BY items must show the author and licence here *and* on the in-game Credits screen.
5. Record any change made (recolour, crop, re-export) in the "Changes" column.
6. Nothing taken from Elden Ring or any other commercial game: assets, names, fonts, UI, music or text.
